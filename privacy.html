Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Postiz Factory ("the app") is a self-hosted tool used only by its operator (GitHub: johnstryder) to publish videos to YouTube channels the operator owns.

What data the app accesses

When the operator connects a YouTube channel, the app uses Google OAuth to access:

  • Basic Google profile information (name, email, profile picture) to identify the connected account.
  • YouTube data for the connected channel: uploading and managing videos, reading channel and video details, and reading YouTube Analytics (views, watch time, retention).

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload and schedule the operator's own videos and to show the operator how those videos perform. It is not used for advertising and is not sold, rented or shared with third parties.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens and channel data are stored on the operator's private server, reachable only over a private network. Data is kept only as long as the channel stays connected.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel to the app, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

YouTube API data stored by the app (channel details, video details and statistics) is refreshed or deleted at least every 30 days, as required by the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.

Revoking access and deletion

You can revoke the app's access to your Google and YouTube data at any time from the Google security settings page (https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions). Removing a channel from the app deletes its stored tokens immediately. All stored data for a channel is deleted within 7 days of access being revoked or of a deletion request sent to jnstrdm05@gmail.com.

Cookies

The app uses a single login cookie for its operator's session. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies and does not collect data from other users or devices.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
