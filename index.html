Postiz Factory

Postiz Factory is a private, self-hosted publishing tool operated by its owner (GitHub: johnstryder). It schedules and uploads videos to the operator's own YouTube channels (such as Reddit Miner and horrorphile) and reads basic channel statistics to decide what to make next.

It is not a public service and is only used by its operator to manage channels they own.

This app uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
